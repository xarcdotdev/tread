Configuration

Personal profile

Used to estimate steps and energy expenditure

Steps use your measured step length, or an approximate 41.4% of your height. Calories use speed-based treadmill MET values and your resting energy. With weight, height, birthdate and male/female gender supplied, resting energy uses Mifflin-St Jeor and age on the report date in the configured timezone. Otherwise the baseline is 1 kcal/kg/hour. Calories include resting energy during walking.

Treadmill

The device controlled by your CLI

Use the same device identifier in every controller config for this treadmill. The initial level must match the belt's actual speed on first start. Commands sent with another remote are not recorded.

Speed calibration

Physical belt speed at each command level

Not calibrated
Speed level Kilometres / hour Metres / second Relative speed
Measure the belt length in metres, count full belt revolutions over a timed interval, then calculate km/h = belt length × revolutions ÷ seconds × 3.6. Write all eight values under [treadmill.speed_kmh], then set calibrated = true under [treadmill]. Updating calibration recalculates historical estimates.

Read-only goal configuration

Goals file: Loading…

Copy goals.example.yaml to goals.yaml beside your server's config.toml. Edit it on the server, then Refresh. Refresh also reloads the personal profile, timezone and speed calibration. No restart is needed.

All periods and targets are optional. Include only what you want to track. Targets must be positive: integer steps, kcal, Go duration strings such as 30m or 1h30m, and average speed in km/h.

daily:
  steps: 1000
  calories: 250
  time: 30m
  average_speed_kmh: 1.5
weekly:
  time: 5h
monthly:
  steps: 150000
yearly:
  time: 250h

Walking time excludes pauses. Average speed is distance divided by walking hours, not an average of levels. Steps need step length or height; calories need weight. Missing speed calibration can make these estimates unavailable. Values above target stay visible; bars stop at 100%.
